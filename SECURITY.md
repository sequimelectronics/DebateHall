# Security

Please report security problems **privately**: use GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button on the
[Security tab](../../security) of this repository, not a public issue. We'll reply as soon as we can.

Helpful to include: what you found, how to reproduce it, and what someone could do with it. Please don't test
against anyone's real Hall except your own.

Especially welcome: anything that lets a seat act as another seat or role, read a huddle or room it isn't in, take
the floor out of turn, reach the keeper's tools, read a paper before it's shared, or get a token out of a Hall.

Debate Hall is a small, self-hosted project, so there's no bug bounty.
