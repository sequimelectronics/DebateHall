## #4 [revise] at 3.3 (Adversary)

**Claim attacked:** Rising sales prove that pineapple improves the eating experience.

**Why:** No figures and no source are given for 'most large chains' or 'risen for a decade'. And sales show that some buyers choose it, not that it improves the meal for eaters generally: an appeal to popularity.

> rising sales prove that pineapple improves the eating experience

**Current proposed text:** Pineapple pizza is widely sold [source needed for chains and sales trend]. That shows steady demand: many buyers enjoy it. It does not show that pineapple improves pizza for eaters generally.

- Advocate, partial: Partly conceded. Sales show demand, not improvement, and the figures need a source. Demand still shows the pairing is enjoyed by a real number of buyers.
  - **Proposed text:** Pineapple pizza is widely sold [source needed for chains and sales trend]. That shows steady demand: many buyers enjoy it. It does not show that pineapple improves pizza for eaters generally.
- Judge, ruling (revise). **What the revision must do:** this claim should be revised to better reflect intent

**Why:** The definition never says for whom. The paper then slides between 'some eaters' (the fan club, buyers) and 'everyone' (most people; any pizza without it is unbalanced). Without a population the central claim cannot be tested. Related: 2.1's tomato-sauce definition quietly excludes white pizzas.

> when it improves the eating experience

(By 'pizza' this paper means tomato-based pizza; white pizzas are outside its scope.)

## #1 [sustained] at 3.2 (Adversary)

**Claim attacked:** The survey shows that most people agree pineapple belongs on pizza.

**Why:** The sample is 40 self-selected members of a pineapple-pizza appreciation group. A fan club's 95% says nothing about people in general; the sample cannot support 'most people' at all. The arithmetic is fine (38/40); the inference is not.

> This shows that most people agree pineapple belongs on pizza.

**Current proposed text:** In a self-selected group of 40 pineapple-pizza fans, 38 said pineapple improves pizza. This reflects fans' views and is not evidence about people in general.

- Advocate, concede: Conceded. A fan club's 95% says nothing about people in general.
  - **Proposed text:** In a self-selected group of 40 pineapple-pizza fans, 38 said pineapple improves pizza. This reflects fans' views and is not evidence about people in general.
- Judge, ruling (sustained). **What the revision must do:** too small a sample, agreed
